About

Marketer, content creator and former competitive boxer. I bring the same discipline I brought to the ring.

I competed as a boxer and represented my university in the ring twice. Boxing taught me to prepare properly, stay composed under pressure and learn from every round, including the ones that don't go my way.

I bring that into marketing. I like ideas that are held accountable by numbers, and I build campaigns the way I train: consistently, and paying attention to what the data says even when it isn't what I hoped to hear.

Outside work, my biggest love is baking. I've made cookies, brookies, brownies and just about every kind of bake you can think of, and I'm always testing the next one. I'm also slowly building a fragrance collection. Both keep me close to how people actually choose the things they love.
